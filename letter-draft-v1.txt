TITLE: YOU ARE INVITED!

Dear Judge,

You have judged a thousand houses from behind a screen. Tonight, come and see one for real.

Mine is small and warm. The Wi-Fi is fast, the coffee is strong, and the comfiest chair in the county has your name on it — I wrote it on a sticky note.

There will be soup. There will be cake. No meetings, no deadlines, and nobody asking you to rate anything.

Come as you are. Bring nothing. Stay as long as you like.

P.S. I know you cannot literally visit — so I drew you a door. Knock twice. The kettle is already on.

— Your host
